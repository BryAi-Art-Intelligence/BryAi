BryAi.website Policy on Data is a written statement on the actions and treatment of data that is, and is not, required from you.

BryAi.website shall not intentionally collect or retain data beyond what is technically essential for providing and securing an internet connection to the website.

You are not required to share any personal details that make you feel uncomfortable.

BryAi will not screenshot or screen-record communications that you send to BryAi. If you choose to screenshot or screen-record those communications, the handling and protection of those copies and the information they contain are your responsibility.

BryAi shall provide a truthful account of the technology used to operate BryAi.website.

BryAi shall not discuss, disclose, or share information provided by you with any other person.

BryAi shall keep read receipts enabled for communications where the feature is available. You are not required to enable read receipts on your communications.

BryAi shall not reuse something made specifically for you for another person, nor provide you with something that was previously made specifically for someone else.

BryAi shall not deny, dismiss, or judge an idea you present based solely on BryAi's personal opinion.

BryAi shall not create art that is prohibited by any laws that may apply.

BryAi shall never initiate contact with you without your prior approval.

BryAi shall not make assumptions or ask questions that are not essential to making progress on your request.

BryAi shall exercise reasonable discretion when determining whether a request can be fulfilled.

BryAi retains discretion to accept or refuse a request, provided that such discretion is exercised consistently with this policy.

BryAi reserves the right to determine what BryAi reasonably considers appropriate under the circumstances.

If BryAi refuses a request, a reason for the refusal shall be provided upon request.
